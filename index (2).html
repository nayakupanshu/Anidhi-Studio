FAQ

Questions we get
all the time.

Do I need a big budget to work with you?

No. Our Starter package begins at Rs. 5,000 — a one-time payment that gets you a professional logo, color system, and font pairing. We built our pricing to be accessible for businesses at every stage, from day-one startups to established brands ready for a refresh.

How long does a project take?

Starter packages take 3 days. Brand Kit takes 5 days. Full Brand System takes 7 days. These are guaranteed delivery timelines from the moment your advance payment is confirmed and your brief is approved.

What if I don't like the designs?

Every package includes at least one revision round. We present multiple concepts first so you can choose the direction that feels right. If something is not right after revisions, we fix it — your satisfaction is the standard we hold ourselves to.

Do you work remotely with businesses anywhere?

Yes. Everything we do is remote-friendly — briefing, presentations, revisions, and delivery all happen over WhatsApp, email, and shared Google Drive links. We work with businesses anywhere in India. Location is never a barrier.

What files will I receive at the end?

You receive your logo in PNG, JPEG, and PDF format. Plus a brand guideline PDF, editable Canva templates, and a color and font reference card — everything in one shared Google Drive folder that you own forever.

How do I pay?

50% advance before work begins, 50% before final files are delivered. We accept UPI, bank transfer, and all major cards via Razorpay. Payment links are sent directly on WhatsApp — simple, fast, and secure.
